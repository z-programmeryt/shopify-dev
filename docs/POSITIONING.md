# Positioning (Phases 5–6)

## Phase 5 — Competitor landscape (summary)

Reviewed: Fiverr Shopify/landing-page sellers surfaced next to Ring Lot's gigs (e.g. dropshipping store builders,
"certified Shopify & Replo" landing-page specialists), Shopify-in-Bangladesh freelancer/agency sites, and GemPages'
2025–2026 "Shopify CRO experts" round-up (agency founders and CRO freelancers who publish case studies).

| What competitors do | What they rarely do |
| --- | --- |
| Lead with big numbers: "200+ pages", "$30M+ brands", "15+ DTC brands", "4+ years" | Show where those numbers come from |
| Claim "certified expert", "10x speed", "high-converting" | Measure speed or conversion before/after |
| Screenshot galleries without context | Explain why a design decision was made |
| Package tiers and urgency | Explain what happens after you contact them |
| CRO language without tracking | Fix tracking first so changes can be evaluated |

Strong CRO operators (e.g. those publishing test-by-test case studies) win on **evidence**, not adjectives.

## Differentiation engine

| Question | Answer for Ring Lot |
| --- | --- |
| What can he prove? | 4.9 from 143 Fiverr reviews · Level 2 · on Fiverr since Dec 2021 · repeat clients (11 of 15 collected reviews) · clients in US, Canada, UAE, Morocco, Mauritania · three corroborated service lines (store builds, landing pages, tracking — tracking 5.0 from 20) |

| What must he not claim? | Client/project counts, revenue, years, certifications, "expert" status, speed multipliers. |

## Phase 6 — Decision

**Professional title: Shopify Developer & Designer.**

| Candidate | Evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify Developer | Store builds, theme customization, "CODE expertise" review | Supported |
| Shopify Designer | Fiverr headline "Shopify Web Designer", portfolio of store designs | Supported |
| Shopify CRO Specialist | Landing pages + tracking, but **no measured CRO outcomes** | Overclaims → used only as a specialization tag ("CRO") and a service |
| eCommerce Developer | True but generic | Weaker for search and recall |
| Conversion-focused Shopify Specialist | Directionally right | Used as descriptor, not title |

**Specialization line:** SHOPIFY / eCOMMERCE / CRO
**Promise (scope, not outcome):** "I design and build Shopify stores and landing pages — and set up the tracking that shows what's working."
**Differentiator:** "Design, build and measurement in one workflow."
