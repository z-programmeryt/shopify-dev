# Content Strategy

## Pillars (from evidenced expertise only)

1. **Tracking & measurement** — Meta Pixel/CAPI, GA4, GTM on Shopify (strongest-reviewed service: 5.0 from 20).
2. **Page builders & landing pages** — GemPages, PageFly, Shogun, theme sections.
3. **Product-page conversion** — structure, proof, variants, delivery information.
4. **Performance** — apps, builders, images, Core Web Vitals.

## Article quality gate (every article must pass)

- Supported? Every factual statement is accurate as of the check date; no invented statistics.
- Expertise? It explains *why*, not just *what*, in an area Ring Lot actually works in (see pillars).
- Useful? A store owner can act on it.
- Original? Not a rewrite of a competitor article.
- Linked? Points to the related service; the service page links back.
- Reviewed? **Ring Lot has reviewed it** (see editorial gate).
